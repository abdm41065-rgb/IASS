01 — The vision

A boutique community of twenty-five homes, designed around light.

MIRA sits on a gently sloping plot between the mountains and the sea, where every residence is oriented to the south-west and the long Mediterranean afternoon. Architecture, landscape and interiors were drawn as a single gesture — low horizontal volumes in lime-washed stone, deep shaded terraces, and gardens that soften the edges of every building.

Only twenty-five families will live here. That number is not a constraint — it is the brief.
